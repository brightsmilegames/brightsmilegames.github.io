Privacy Policy – Hold the City

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Hold the City (the "Game"), published by WALID IDELMOU under the name BrightSmile Games ("we", "us"), handles information when you play. We have designed the Game to collect as little information as possible.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game has no account and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal information, and we do not run our own servers that receive your data.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, upgrades, settings and similar data) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and how you interact with ads. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalized.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from the Game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Learn how Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases (coin packs and "No Ads") are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, so the Game can deliver it. See the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Unity services

The Game is built with Unity and uses Unity's in-app purchase service, which may process technical information about your device (such as device model and operating system) to operate. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, most requests are handled through your device settings and through Google. You can also contact us at any time.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date of the last update at the top.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at brightsmile.games@gmail.com.
